# Roadmap

Each session builds on the last. Movement and gunplay come first, because everything else depends on them feeling good.

## ✅ Session 1: Gun range and movement (build 0.1)
- Renderer: physical sky, IBL, cascaded shadows, PBR, post.
- Movement: sprint, tactical sprint, crouch, slide, mantle.
- Three guns with bullet drop, penetration, recoil patterns and dynamic spread.
- Steel targets, strafing bots, wallbang lane, movement course.
- HUD, synthesized audio, settings menu, single-file share build.

## Session 2: First real map, loot and bots
- A larger map chunk: a small town with enterable buildings, windows, rooftops and a hill.
- Loot: ground loot with rarities, armour plates (plate up with a key), ammo types, cash.
- Bots that move, take cover, peek, shoot back and push.
- Player health, armour, downs and self-revive.

## Session 3: The battle royale loop
- Plane drop with parachute.
- Closing gas zone with phases.
- Win / lose screens and a match summary.
- A **Gulag**-style 1v1 second chance.
- Buy stations: loadout drop, armour, self-revive, UAV.

## Session 4: More guns and the gunsmith
- 10+ guns: marksman rifle, LMG, shotgun, pistol, launcher.
- Attachments with real trade-offs: optics, muzzles, barrels, stocks, grips, mags.
- Blueprints and a loadout editor.

## Session 5: Progression
- Player XP and levels, plus weapon levels that unlock attachments.
- Camo challenges (headshots, longshots, wallbangs…) and mastery camos.
- Operators, calling cards and a battle-pass-style track, saved locally.

## Session 6: Vehicles and multiplayer
- Vehicles: ATV, SUV and helicopter.
- Online play: an authoritative Node.js server over WebSockets, client-side prediction and lag compensation for hit registration, squads.
